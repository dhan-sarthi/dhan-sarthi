# ADR-0007 — Product shelf, holdings and policies stay fixture-sourced behind a composite adapter until IDBI confirms endpoints

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-03

## Context

The numbered IDBI sandbox catalogue described in `docs/integration/data-requirements.md` exposes
the customer master, accounts, statement lines, overdues and liens. It has no endpoint for
mutual-fund holdings, insurance policies or the product shelf, and the suitability rules need
all three. Blocking the demo on the bank would leave nothing to show; pretending the catalogue
has them would mislead the people reviewing the integration.

## Decision

`ProductShelfPort` is separate from `BankDataPort`. `CompositeBankData` answers customer,
accounts, transactions and liabilities from the IDBI stub and holdings and policies from the
secondary adapter, stamping provenance per block that Record → Your data displays.

## Alternatives

- Pretend the catalogue has the missing endpoints.
- Block the demo on the bank.

## Consequences

"IDBI has no endpoint for X" is a labelled state, not a surprise; the day the bank exposes one,
the composite drops a branch.
