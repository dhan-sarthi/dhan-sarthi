# ADR-0006 — Append-only, hash-chained record; audit rows carry subject_id, never cif

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-03

## Context

The product's credibility inside a bank rests on the record: for every proposal, the exact
sentence shown, the rule that produced it, the snapshot it was computed from and the customer's
decision. That record has to be immutable at the database, not by convention, and it has to be
kept for years. At the same time, DPDP erasure has to be real. An immutable row that carries the
customer identifier contradicts a right to erasure; deleting audit rows contradicts retention.
The two requirements have to be reconciled in the schema.

## Decision

`advice_records`, `decisions` and `avatar_tool_calls` are INSERT-only for `dhan_app`, with
`BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE` triggers that RAISE. `record_hash = sha256(prev_hash ‖ canonical(row))`
per session; `pnpm audit:verify` walks the chain. Audit rows reference
`subjects(subject_id, cif)`; DPDP erasure deletes the subjects row and cascades sessions and
snapshots, leaving audit rows immutable and unlinkable.

## Alternatives

- Immutability by convention.
- Storing `cif` on audit rows and pseudonymising in place. This contradicts the trigger.
- Deleting audit rows on erasure. This contradicts five-year retention.

## Consequences

Decision B4 in `docs/product/decisions.md` is enforced by the database; erasure and retention are
reconciled without touching a record row; a tampered row is detectable with one script.
