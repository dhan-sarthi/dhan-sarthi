# PS1 Research Brief (12 Jul 2026)

Compiled via web research for the Dhan Sarthi submission. Full sources inline.

## 1. IDBI Bank context
- FY26: 2,243 outlets, deposits ₹3.47L Cr (+23%), record PAT ₹9,513 Cr (+27%), GNPA 2.32% — healthy and cash-rich.
- **Retail ~61% of book** (Q3 FY26, up from 52% FY22); RAM ~72% of advances; non-interest income ~28% already leans on bancassurance/MF fees.
- **IDBI GO Mobile+**: 11 languages, UPI, FD/RD, digital loans — but **no robo-advisory, no goal-based investing, no wealth view, no conversational assistant**. ~92% of retail transactions digital.
- Wealth offerings today: third-party MF distribution (pure distributor), bancassurance, RM-led service for affluent only. IDBI's AMC was sold to **LIC Mutual Fund** → LIC MF is the in-family manufacturer.
- **LIC/privatization**: LIC took majority in 2019 (now ~30.24% + Govt 30.48%, divesting 60.72%; bids under evaluation Feb 2026, ~₹33,000 Cr). IDBI is **LIC's #1 bancassurance partner**. Post-privatization, fee income + stickiness is the strategic story.
- Digital initiatives 2024-26: AI SOC, blockchain trade finance, OCEN/GeM Sahay, AWS engagement — nothing customer-facing in AI wealth.

## 2. Competitors
- **HDFC SmartWealth (2024)**: risk-profiled model portfolios, goal "SmartJars", consolidated external view, 3-min onboarding. Benchmark for table stakes. No avatar, no conversation.
- **SBI YONO**: 8.8 Cr users; Jul 2026 launches "YONO Ji" agentic assistant (service) and "Financial Fitness" wellbeing score (scores, doesn't advise).
- **Kotak Cherry**: curated investing under registered IA. **ICICI**: RM-led wealth + iPal service bot. **INDIE**: neobank UX, DIY investing.
- Fintechs: INDmoney (aggregation, not fiduciary advice), Jupiter, Fi (wound down neo-banking 2025-26 — strengthens embed-in-bank thesis), Groww ~1.3 Cr / Zerodha ~78L active — execution platforms, advice-light.
- **Avatars**: Bank of Baroda **"Aditi"** (Sep 2024) — GenAI human-like avatar RM, audio/video/chat, multilingual — but a *service* bot, not wealth advisory. HDFC Eva, Axis AHA, SBI ILA: text/voice service bots.
- **Gap: no Indian bank combines a persistent visual avatar with regulated, personalized wealth advisory in its main app.**

## 3. Regulatory
- **SEBI IA Regulations 2013** (amended to Nov 2025): robo-advice = full IA compliance parity. Mandatory risk profiling before advice, client consent on profile, suitability assessment, **5-year records**; proposals: tools "fit for purpose", disclosed workings, audited.
- **SEBI Intermediaries (Amendment) Regulations 2025** (Feb 2025): regulated entities **fully responsible for AI outputs**; must **disclose extent of AI usage to clients**.
- **SEBI consultation (Jun 2025)**: Responsible AI/ML guidelines — governance policy, model registration, 5-yr model-parameter retention, independent testing.
- **RBI Account Aggregator/Sahamati**: **25.29 Cr users, 2.61 Bn linked accounts (Dec 2025)**; covers banks, insurers, RTAs, depositories → consolidated consented net-worth view.
- **DPDP Act 2023 + Rules (Nov 2025)**: purpose-linked granular consent; banks likely Significant Data Fiduciaries; penalties to ₹250 Cr. Face photos for future-self rendering need explicit consent.

## 4. Behavioral finance (avatar's scientific backbone)
- **Hershfield et al., JMR 2011** (DOI 10.1509/jmkr.48.SPL.S23): age-progressed self-renderings → **~2× allocation to long-term savings** (immersive condition); working adults +30% of pay to retirement. Mechanism: future self-continuity.
- **Merrill Edge "Face Retirement" (BofA, 2012)**: productized the research as a marketing microsite. Our angle: first *persistent, in-app, regulated* future-self advisor; not productized in India.

## 5. Market stats (deck-ready)
1. SIP inflows ₹31–32k Cr/month through H1 2026 (record ₹32,087 Cr Mar 2026); SIP AUM ₹16.85L Cr; industry AUM ~₹81.6L Cr.
2. ~12.7 Cr unique equity investors (~9% of population); only ~3–5% in mutual funds; demat penetration ~12% vs ~62% US.
3. Wealth AUM US$1.1T (FY24) → ~US$2.3T by FY29 (Deloitte); 30–40% of affluent take no professional advice.
4. "Missing middle" (₹2.5–13L income): ~5–6% MF penetration; B30 cities = 50%+ of new folios but only ~18.9% of AUM.

## One-line synthesis
IDBI is a retail-heavy, newly profitable, soon-to-be-privatized bank with LIC's bancassurance engine and no digital wealth product — competitors shipped avatars (Aditi) or wealth apps (SmartWealth) but never both; AA rails + SEBI's AI framework + Hershfield's evidence make an avatar-led, compliance-native advisor buildable and defensible in 2026.
