# The 3-minute demo video

**The single decision that makes this video good: everything serves the refusal.**

You have ~180 seconds and a jury that will watch a dozen of these. Most will be a feature
tour. Yours should be one story with a turn in it: *the app finds money, the customer asks
to buy something, and the bank's own app says no and names something cheaper.* Nothing
else in the field will do that.

**Spend 50 seconds on the refusal.** Everything before it exists to make it land;
everything after it exists to prove it was not a scripted string.

Record on **Karan** for the money, switch to **Rohan** for the refusal — Rohan's refusal is
rule 9 (bundled protection), which fires with no debt excuse, so it reads as the bank
refusing its own product on the merits. Karan's is rule 1, which a cynic can dismiss as
"of course, he has debt."

## The avatar take — read this before you record

> ⚠️ **Runway credits are the constraint on this shoot.** A live call bills ~2 credits up
> front plus ~2 per 6 seconds. The 50-second take in this script costs **≈19 credits**, and
> you will want **three takes**, so budget **≈60**. The agreed 100-credit test cap has 12
> left. **Top up, or raise the cap deliberately, before the shoot day — do not discover
> this mid-record.** Check the balance before and after: `GET /v1/organization` →
> `creditBalance`.

Plan the take:
- **Rehearse the question with the mic off.** A session that is created but never consumed
  costs 0 credits — you can practise the UI path for free
- **Ask it exactly as scripted.** This is the question the personality brief and the
  suitability tool were built around; improvising invites a turn where the model answers
  from its own judgement instead of calling the tool
- **Do not cut Uday off.** The refusal sentence is the whole video. Let it finish
- **Record audio from the tab, not the room mic.** Uday's voice must be clean
- If the call fails on the day: the **fallback is not a disaster, it is a feature.** Show
  the three-tier ladder — "Uday is with another customer", text refusal, same sentence,
  same rule, same record. Say that out loud: *"the advice does not depend on the avatar
  being up."* A jury that sees a graceful degradation trusts the system more than one that
  sees a perfect take

## Shooting notes

- **Screen-record the real app on a real phone**, or the Expo web target at 390×844 in a
  phone frame. Do not animate screenshots — the app has motion (count-ups, staggered
  entrances, the plan re-cutting) and it is worth more than any transition you would add
- **No background music under the avatar.** Music elsewhere is fine, low
- **Show the ₹ figures long enough to read.** ₹34,65,599 and ₹1,86,240 each want ~2 seconds
- **One cut per beat.** Eight beats, eight cuts. Resist a montage
- **Subtitle the avatar's refusal**, burned in. If the jury watches on mute, that sentence
  is the one thing they must still read
- **3:00 is a hard ceiling.** If you are over, cut from 0:12–0:35 (onboarding), never from
  1:25–2:30 (the refusal)

## What to leave out

Genuinely good work that does not earn its seconds here: the challenges, save hacks, the
credit view, the spend charts, the budget pane, the statement enrichment. They are slide-10
material. A video that shows everything shows nothing.
