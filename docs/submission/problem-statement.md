## Official problem statement (from the Hack2skill portal)
**Title:** Problem Statement 1: Digital Wealth Management
**Tag:** Wealth Advisory

> Wealth management and advisory services remain fragmented and largely inaccessible to a large number of customers. Absence of comprehensive [insight into] customer investment behaviour and spending habits limits the ability to provide timely, personalized, data-driven guidance.
>
> **Expected Outcome:** Bank is looking for an AI-powered Digital Wealth Management (Avatar-Based) application which integrates into the bank's mobile application, to deliver personalized and scalable wealth advisory services through an intuitive digital interface.
